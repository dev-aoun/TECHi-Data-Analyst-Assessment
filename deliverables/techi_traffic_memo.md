# TECHi Traffic Assessment: Is Traffic Actually Up?

**To:** TECHi Management  
**Subject:** Traffic growth, section performance, and measurement reliability  
**Period:** September 2025–August 2026 | **Sample:** 120 article records

## Executive Summary

The available data does not prove TECHi traffic is up 30% year over year (YoY). The dataset spans September 2025–August 2026 without the prior-year baseline required for a direct YoY comparison. Crucially, reported sessions jumped 47.95% between January and February 2026—coinciding with a site-wide analytics snippet change. This step-change is consistent with a possible measurement break rather than genuine audience growth. Until the tracking implementation is independently verified, the increase cannot confidently be attributed to real audience growth.

From March to August 2026, reported sessions grew from 96,570 to 102,805, up 6.46% (1.26% monthly compound growth). This is the clearest post-change trend, though not a verified full-year growth estimate.

## A. Is the "Up 30% YoY" Claim True?

Unverified. Reported January–August traffic rose 60.63%, but this crosses the February tracking change, where all six sections jumped sharply (47.95% overall). Pre-February sessions averaged 63,580/month versus 98,186/month afterward. Because these periods span a suspected tracking break, they cannot be treated as comparable audience levels.

## B. Best Estimate of Underlying Change

The most defensible descriptive metric is the March–August gain of 6.46%, showing modest growth after the February step-change. Without a tracking audit, true underlying growth remains uncertain; report the post-February trend separately rather than citing an unverified YoY figure.

Additionally, November 2025 Crypto traffic spiked 145.54% (from 10,100 to 24,800) due to a 36-hour aggregator front-page feature—an isolated event, not recurring growth.

## C. Should Guides Be Retired?

Not immediately. Guides fell 85.29% from March (4,930) to August (725), alongside minimal publishing since March and negligible session-based ad revenue. However, the data lacks editorial costs, newsletter attribution, and search value.

Pause major new investment, refresh high-value evergreen guides, and measure their search and newsletter contribution before deciding on permanent closure. Keeping Guides risks ongoing editorial opportunity cost; retiring it prematurely risks losing evergreen search traffic and subscriber acquisition.

## Newsletter and Limitations

The `newsletter_signups` field increased 39.02% (820 to 1,140), but its definition (monthly new vs. cumulative) is unconfirmed. Furthermore, the current article scrape cannot verify historical monthly publishing volume.

## Recommendation and Next Evidence

Audit the February 2026 analytics change against an independent source, keep the 30% YoY claim unverified until like-for-like data exists, and place Guides under a measured review period.
